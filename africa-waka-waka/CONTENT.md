### Sierra Leone – Tourism, 7 Nights · $1,750
- $1,750 per room for 2 adults · children $750 · deposit $300
- 7 days and 7 nights; family friendly; pure assisted/guided tourism
- Day 1: land at Freetown International Airport (Lungi), met by the team, ~10-minute drive to Africa Waka Waka's Home Style Hotel
- Day 2: ferry to Freetown in the VIP cabin (mountains, city skyline, ocean); check in at Mamba Point Hotel (preferred hotel partner) at the Aberdeen beach area
- Day 3: more Freetown + second Kekeh Tour™; choose beaches: River Number 2, Bureh, Tokeh, Aberdeen; nightlife around Aberdeen Beach
- Day 4: Mozza Resort, total relaxation; drive along the Peninsula Highway with ocean and mountain views
- Day 5: floating day: another day at Mozza, back to Mamba Point, or Riptide Sunset Resort in Freetown
- Day 6: farewell activities in Lungi: last night at the Home Style Hotel, fashion and music performances (season dependent), goodbyes to the team
- Day 7: departure
- Included: all transportation in Sierra Leone · all lodging · security and personal assistance · 2 days + 6 hours of Kekeh Tour · ticket to the nation's chimpanzee zoo · beach hopping · nightlife hopping · activities within the Africa Waka Waka hotel · daily breakfast
- Payment: full payment due within 14 days of the trip start date; deposit option sends a payment link for the balance
- Economy, Gold or Diamond package options: Africana clothing custom-made for you, Kekeh Tours through Freetown, beach life, heritage-site exploration, participation in religious programs, volunteer work, entertainment industry, hotel-hopping and more
