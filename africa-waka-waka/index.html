Day 1 → Day 7

Day 1

Land in Lungi

Arrive at Freetown International Airport. Our team meets you and it’s a ten-minute drive to Africa Waka Waka’s home-style hotel.

Day 2

The VIP ferry to Freetown

Cross to the capital in the ferry’s VIP cabin, with views of the mountains, the city skyline and the ocean. Check in at Mamba Point Hotel, our preferred partner on the corner of the Aberdeen beach area.

Day 3

Kekeh Tour™ & beaches

More of Freetown and your second Kekeh Tour. Choose your beaches: River Number 2, Bureh, Tokeh or Aberdeen. At night, the Aberdeen beach area comes alive.

Day 4

Mozza Resort

Total relaxation at one of the most beautiful resorts in Sierra Leone, via the Peninsula Highway and its incredible views of ocean and mountains.

Day 5

Floating day

More fun and exploration, your way: a second day at Mozza, back to Mamba Point, or an evening at Riptide Sunset Resort in Freetown.

Day 6

Farewell in Lungi

Back to the home-style hotel for your last night: fashion and music performances (they vary by season) and goodbyes to the team who looked after you.

Day 7

Fly home

Eight minutes to the terminal and a free shuttle to your flight, with memories to last until the next trip.

Our story

An oasis of hope and prosperity.

Founder TY Kanu invested in tourism and hospitality because it is one of Sierra Leone’s biggest opportunities for shared prosperity. His own journeys home showed him the gaps, and Africa Waka Waka is his answer to them.

Lungi was chosen for our first resort because it is the gateway: every international visitor lands here, and it is a key place to build real tourism infrastructure. Most of our team are from Lungi, and every stay supports meaningful local work.

Our vision is a robust infrastructure for tourism in Sierra Leone and around Africa, one that can be used for:

  1. Setting standards within the tourism & hospitality sector
  2. Improving education through international study-abroad programs
  3. Facilitating business conferences and events
  4. Community outreach programs
